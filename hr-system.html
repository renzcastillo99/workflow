Leave policy

Employees with 1 year of service or more get the yearly amount (it renews on each hire anniversary, with no carry-over). New hires earn a set amount for every completed month until their first anniversary.

Backup & restore

Your data lives in this browser. If you clear your browser data it is gone, so download a backup now and then.
